### ISSUE-011: Combat tiles composited a white `card_shadow` and placed cost off the pip

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Severity** | high |
| **Category** | visual |
| **URL** | `/compendium/sts1/cards` |

**Description:** `512/card_shadow` is a white silhouette. The game does not draw it under library cards (`renderImage` uses `FRAME_SHADOW_COLOR` black 0.25 on the bg, hover uses `CARD_SUPER_SHADOW`). Lossy WebP also stored white RGB on `a=0` pixels, which bilinear-scaled into a white haze. Cost CSS (`left: 2%; width: 18%`) sat to the right of the energy pip. Game text is `FontHelper.renderRotatedText(current_x, current_y, -132, 192)` on the 300×420 body.

**Repro Steps:**

1. Open `/compendium/sts1/cards`
2. Look at 타격 tiles against the dark page
3. **Observe (before):** white rectangular haze; cost digit right of the orb
4. **Observe (after):** no white outer ring (pixel sample of Strike tile outer ring = 0 near-white pixels); digit centroid matches the fire core (±1px) and the game offset
