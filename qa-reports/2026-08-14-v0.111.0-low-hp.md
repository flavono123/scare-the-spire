## Issues

### ISSUE-001: Character Low HP checkbox vanishes with `weak_loop` Spine error

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Severity** | critical |
| **Category** | console / functional |
| **URL** | http://localhost:3000/compendium/characters/ironclad |

**Description:** Character detail loaded a cached `/generated/compendium-detail-kor.json` whose Ironclad `spineAsset.animations` listed `weak_loop`. That name is not in the skeleton (`low_health_loop` is). Spine Player treats `config.animations` as a required allow-list, throws uncaught after `showError`, and the Low HP checkbox appears then disappears. Hard refresh after regenerating the payload was required until the fetch used `cache: "no-cache"`.

**Repro Steps:**

1. Open `/compendium/characters/ironclad` with a stale cached Compendium detail JSON that lists `weak_loop`.
2. Wait for Spine to load.
3. **Observe:** Console `Error: Animation in config list does not exist in skeleton: weak_loop`; Low HP checkbox gone; HP stays 80/80.
