## Roadmap Revisited

Outside of our ongoing tasks of bug fixes and balance updates, many items on our roadmap have started seeing some movement:

### Things that have been added

- Steam Workshop support
- Initial scaffolding for the Bestiary
- The badge/score system
- Traditional Chinese and Indonesian localization
- Lots of new cards, relics, etc.

### Things we're actively working on

- Experimental game modes
- Alternate Act 2
- New character

Our bi-weekly update cadence on the beta branch has allowed us to cross quite a few features and content off of our to-do list. It also allows us to be more dynamic and experimental when introducing changes. However, when doling out content piecemeal like that, it's harder to dedicate time to developing the bigger, more impactful content that so many players are most eager for. As you can see above, we've since shifted our focus to such pieces of content, including an alternate Act 2 and new character! In order to bring you this much-anticipated content, we will be shifting our updates to be a bit further apart, at least for a while. We can't guarantee the exact pace or if said pace will be regular, but we'll continue to keep you updated, as always!

While we're really excited to show off everything we've been working on, we also hate spoilers. So please take this foot for now.

Time for our (roughly bi-monthly) Q&A section! If you'd like your question to be featured here, the best way to send it to us is fanmail@megacrit.com.

Clapah asks: I am loving Slay the Spire 2! I was thinking about the balance of [gold:ascension]Ascension 10[/gold], knowing it is a fair bit easier than Ascension 20 in Spire 1. How difficult are you aiming for [gold:ascension]Ascension 10[/gold] to be? Will it be around Ascension 20 in Spire 1 or closer to how it is now in Early Access?

Casey: Our general thought has been that [gold:ascension]Ascension 10[/gold] is close to the highest difficulty we'll have for the game. As more content makes it into the game, there will be a clearer picture on how we want to tackle challenging difficulty though :D

<!-- claim:enchantments -->
SuperJuan2000 asks: Enchantments have been my favorite addition to this game, they help spruce up cards and make the game feel endlessly fun. Do you have any plans for more enchantments? Additionally, would you guys add in enchant stacking in the future (as an example, the [gold:event]Self-Help Book[/gold] event can grant +2 damage on a card and then picking up [gold:relic]Gnarled Hammer[/gold] grants +3 damage, stacking up to +5 damage since they're the same enchantment)?

Casey: We like enchantments too! However, we explicitly disallow stacking of enchantments as the probability of snowballing with a single card goes up. We want our players to experience deckbuilding rather than cardbuilding!

## The Annual Stopwatch — Cat Food Cup

Did you know that every year, the Chinese StS community holds at least one tournament called the Cat Food Cup, hosted by [IrisRaphael](https://space.bilibili.com/8831648)? And right now, as this Neowsletter reaches your hands, it's getting ready to raise the curtain on the Top 16 of the main bracket over on bilibili!

This is already the 7th Cat Food Cup, and the core rules of this tournament — passed down all the way from Slay the Spire 1 — have never changed: each player gets two SL (save-load) chances, then two players run the exact same seed, and whoever climbs further and faster up the Spire takes the win. You can find the full schedule from Aug 8th to Aug 30th and detailed competition rules [right here](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ht3m6UEc9/)! There will be guest appearances from various streamers, so go check out the matches! Also, shoutout to 鸟蛋 for making the event's cool logo!
