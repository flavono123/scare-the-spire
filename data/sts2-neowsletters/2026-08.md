## August patch

New content is at least a month away from the beta branch. The team is off in late August, so there is no August 27 patch.

<!-- claim:roadmap -->
## Roadmap

Already in:

- Steam Workshop support
- Initial scaffolding for the Bestiary
- The badge/score system
- Traditional Chinese and Indonesian localization
- New cards and relics

In progress:

- Experimental game modes
- An alternate Act 2
- A new character

Bi-weekly beta updates cleared a lot of the list, and left less time for the larger content. The focus has moved to that work, including an alternate Act 2 and a new character, so updates will be farther apart for a while. The pace is not guaranteed to be regular.

They would rather not spoil it, so the only reveal for now is a foot.

## Ascension 10

Clapah asked whether [gold:ascension]Ascension 10[/gold], which is much easier than Ascension 20 in the first game, is meant to land near that Ascension 20 or stay close to the current Early Access difficulty.

Casey: [gold:ascension]Ascension 10[/gold] is close to the highest difficulty planned for this game. Grinding out Ascension 20 would be a chore with more characters. More content will make the plan for harder difficulty clearer.

<!-- claim:enchantments -->
## Enchantment stacking

SuperJuan2000 asked whether more enchantments are planned, and whether the same enchantment can stack. The example was [gold:event]Self-Help Book[/gold] granting +2 damage and [gold:relic]Gnarled Hammer[/gold] granting +3, for +5 total.

Casey: Enchantments are welcome, and stacking them is explicitly disallowed because one card would be more likely to snowball. The goal is deckbuilding, not cardbuilding.

## Cat Food Cup

The Chinese community's Cat Food Cup, hosted by IrisRaphael, is on its 7th edition. The rules carried over from the first game are unchanged: two save-load chances each, then both players run the same seed, and the one who climbs farther and faster wins. The Top 16 runs from August 8 through August 30. The bracket and broadcasts are on the bilibili link in the original post.
