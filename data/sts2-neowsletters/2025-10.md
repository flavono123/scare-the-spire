## The Necrobinder

At long last, the time has come to dive deeper into the first new character we announced for Slay the Spire 2: [gold:character]The Necrobinder[/gold], a sassy lich who relies upon a giant, reanimated hand called [gold]Osty[/gold] in battle. (It’s a funny name but not technically a pun, so should be safe.)

![The Necrobinder with giant companion Osty attacking with Poke](/images/neowsletters/2025-10/osty-poke.jpg)

![Common attack card Bodyguard and Uncommon attack card Bone Shards](/images/neowsletters/2025-10/bodyguard-boneshards.jpg)

Her kit is themed around necromancy, fittingly enough, and plays with the balance of life and death. Osty can attack enemies independently of the Necrobinder and is unaffected by debuffs afflicting her. Faithful companion that he is, Osty also takes attack damage that would normally affect the Necrobinder… as long as he has HP to give, that is. To increase Osty’s HP or revive him from the dead once more, the Necrobinder can [gold]Summon[/gold] him. Your strategy may see you building up a high HP Osty for a constant source of protection or sacrificing him only to bring him back from the dead over and over again.

<!-- claim:necrobinder-doom -->
### Doom

![Common attack card Blight Strike and Rare power card Neurosurge](/images/neowsletters/2025-10/blightstrike-neurosurge.jpg)

Another key component of the Necrobinder’s kit is [gold]Doom[/gold], which we have teased in previous Neowsletters. When a creature’s Doom meets or exceeds its current HP, it will die. It’s a debuff that’s applied similarly to [gold:power]Poison[/gold], but it does not deal damage over time or tick down, and it only affects creatures at the end of their turn, as opposed to the start.

![A knight-like enemy dying to Doom](/images/neowsletters/2025-10/doomed-knight.jpg)

<!-- claim:necrobinder-souls -->
### Souls & Transformation

![Common skill card Soul and Common Skill card Grave Warden](/images/neowsletters/2025-10/soul-gravewarden.jpg)

She can also use her necromantic magic to draw (ha) on the power of [gold]Souls[/gold], giving her a source of card draw.

![Common attack Sculpting Strike and Rare skill card Transfigure](/images/neowsletters/2025-10/sculptingstrike-transfigure.jpg)

Transformation is a major part of necromancy, of course, so the Necrobinder also has many cards that allow her to alter other cards, applying different keywords to them and changing how they play.

That’s all the information on our little lich that we have for you today, but there will be plenty more to uncover still.
