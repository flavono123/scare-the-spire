### Crossovers Galore

The moment has finally arrived… The [gold:character]Watcher[/gold] joins the fray!!

In case you don’t know, [Fraymakers](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1420350/Fraymakers/) is a fighting game with a roster of iconic indie characters, among which, we are excited to say, now includes our very own [gold:character]Watcher[/gold] from Slay the Spire! Check out The Watcher’s [release trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LLyl-7NAd-Q) to see her in action.

<!-- claim:crossover-spiritfall -->
But that’s not all, action roguelite [Spiritfall](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1835240/Spiritfall/) just dropped its Crossover Cosmetics patch, which includes a skin of The [gold:character]Ironclad[/gold] as well as characters from other indie titles! His aesthetic fits right into the world of Spiritfall, we gotta say.

![The three skins added to Spiritfall, including Ironclad from Slay the Spire](/images/neowsletters/2024-08/spiritfall-skins.jpg)

But that’s also not all! There may be yet another Slay the Spire easter egg in a game launching later this week, one with a totally different genre and art style from Slay the Spire… can you guess which one?

### Q&A Corner

In this section we’ll be taking your most pressing questions and answering them 🔴LIVE!

That was a lie, we’re not answering them live. Many of you have asked questions since the showcase in April, and we’ve compiled a list of answers for the most frequently asked questions–a FAQ, one might say–on our website [here](https://megacrit.com/faq)!

If you still have questions (that aren’t support-related), you can send them our way via the #ask-the-devs channel in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/slaythespire) and we’ll get to it as soon as we can!

We’re trying our best to be transparent but our release is still a bit far off so not all of our details are concrete and, more importantly, we don’t want to spoil things for you. Any story-related spoilers will be SPOILER TAGGED in the future.
