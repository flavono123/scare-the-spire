## The Regent

![An aloof, orange-colored alien with a diamond/star-shaped head sitting on a stone throne](/images/neowsletters/2025-12/regent.jpg)

Hailing from a distant planet, [gold:character]The Regent[/gold] has traveled long and far on his quest for excitement that his homeworld cannot offer. His journey has of course taken him to the Spire, full of strange, formidable creatures to conquer and innumerable treasure to plunder. Heir to the Throne of Stars, he is a domineering and arrogant being who draws on the power of the cosmos and his long-suffering minions to do his bidding.

<!-- claim:regent-stars -->
### Stars

![Two cards: Hidden Cache and Falling Star](/images/neowsletters/2025-12/regent-star.jpg)

The most fundamental aspect of [gold:character]The Regent[/gold]’s kit is that he has another resource that some of his cards rely on besides energy: [gold]Stars[/gold]. After generating Stars, they can then be spent on certain cards that require them to deal damage, block, and empower himself with. Some cards even scale with the Stars you have stored up. Stars do not deplete at the end of your turn and there is no cap on how many you can have at once, so the sky is the limit.

<!-- claim:regent-minions -->
### Minions

![Two cards: GUARDS!!! and Minion Sacrifice](/images/neowsletters/2025-12/regent-minion.jpg)

[gold:character]The Regent[/gold] is also capable of transforming other cards into unceasingly loyal [gold]Minions[/gold] to attack and defend for him. Formed of celestial material, you will also find these disposable but adorable Minions painstakingly lugging [gold:character]The Regent[/gold] around on his giant throne.

<!-- claim:regent-forge -->
### Forge

![Two cards: Summon Forth and Sovereign Blade](/images/neowsletters/2025-12/regent-forge.jpg)

As the heir to his family’s empire, [gold:character]The Regent[/gold] has the ability to [gold]Forge[/gold] his own signature weapon, the [gold:card]Sovereign Blade[/gold], a large, gleaming sword that orbits around him. The first time you play a card with the “[gold]Forge[/gold]” keyword each combat, the [gold:card]Sovereign Blade[/gold]’s corresponding card will be created. Every time you Forge after that, the amount of Forge increases the amount of damage [gold:card]Sovereign Blade[/gold] will deal. It has [gold]Retain[/gold] by default, so you can continue to build up your Blade to be as lethal as possible until the exact right moment.

<!-- claim:regent-colorless -->
### Colorless Synergy

![Two cards: Spectrum Shift and Supermassive](/images/neowsletters/2025-12/regent-colorless.jpg)

Something else that is unique to this character is his synergy with colorless cards, which he can both create and interact with in interesting ways. You may have noticed that [gold:card]Sovereign Blade[/gold] is a colorless card, so this opens up a whole new avenue of Forge-based builds to explore, as well.

Overall, [gold:character]The Regent[/gold] is a character that revolves around your ability to manage his resources and scale up his power. What exactly his backstory is and what his future entails will be unveiled in due time…

## Q&A with C&A

<!-- claim:qa-3d -->
The Singaporean Smoked Machine asks: Are there any ideas that you have decided to completely scrap in STS2 that you could tell us?

Casey: There was a brief exploration of 3D but it was scrapped because the value add wasn’t high and Anthony and I are more into games with a lot of content rather than flashy stuff. Sorry flashy lovers, it’s just a lot easier to create and iterate content if they’re 2D assets.

<!-- claim:qa-delayed -->
BaySharp asks: Since STS2 got delayed for a fair amount of time, will we get any new gameplay trailer to compensate for it? Or perhaps, new gameplay footage? Or a demo even? Or just pretty much anything that will bring more hype to the game before the release?

Casey: Yes, we publish a monthly newsletter (The Neowsletter) with sneak peeks and stuff. We even answer questions on there! Also, you may want to keep a particularly close eye out for something happening in February, closer to the launch…
