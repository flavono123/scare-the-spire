## The Evolution of Slay the Spire 2’s Content

Since it’s been over a year since we started teasing StS2 content, we figured now is a good time to check back in on what some of it looks like now. We did warn you it was all subject to change! Things will evolve even further with all of your feedback throughout early access, of course, but we figured we’d let you get some last minute speculation in.

<!-- claim:evolution-gold-axe -->
![A before and after image of Gold Axe](/images/neowsletters/2026-01/gold-axe-buff.jpg)

One of the very first new cards we teased back in November of 2024 was [gold:card]Gold Axe[/gold]. While it excited people even in its initial state, it’s since seen a buff with the removal of its Exhaust keyword and a reduction in its Energy cost. Not pictured: we still have shiny VFX based on the rarity of card rewards, though it has been toned down from what you see on the left.

<!-- claim:evolution-rolling-boulder -->
![A before and after image of Rolling Boulder](/images/neowsletters/2026-01/rolling-boulder-nerf.jpg)

On the other hand, [gold:card]Rolling Boulder[/gold], a card we teased this past July was indeed seeming a bit too OP. Its base damage and scaling damage have now both been slashed in half. We’ll see what you all think of it soon enough–that is if it doesn’t get changed again in the meantime.

<!-- claim:evolution-bodyguard-unleash -->
![A comparison image of Bodyguard and Unleash](/images/neowsletters/2026-01/bodyguard-unleash.jpg)

This [gold:card]Bodyguard[/gold] card that we showed off this past October was actually [gold:character]The Necrobinder[/gold]’s unique starter card, but it has since been totally reworked. While it has the same art, it now goes by a new name, [gold:card]Unleash[/gold], and has a completely different mechanic. Perhaps we’ll see the old card again in some form or another… and perhaps the new card will undergo its own evolution someday.

<!-- claim:evolution-repy -->
![A comparison image of the old War Historian, Repy event and the updated one](/images/neowsletters/2026-01/repy-change.jpg)

On a slightly different note, the placeholder event art that we teased for our war historian friend [gold:event]War Historian, Repy[/gold] has been updated (as well as the event text and options)! The original art certainly had its own charm, though. RIP beta Repy, gone but not forgotten.
