## Relics & Potions

Here’s an assortment of relics (top row) and potions (bottom row) coming in the sequel! Relics retain their classic look but you may have noticed that potions got a little makeover; they each have their own distinct sprites. Very rare potions can look quite exotic too.

![A series of various new relics and potions](/images/neowsletters/2025-08/relics-potions.gif)

- Wongo’s Bargain Ticket: Here we see a standard bargain ticket from a Wongo’s. Only patrons of Wongo’s can find out what this means.
- [gold:relic]Stone Humidifier[/gold]: This handy little device ensures that you gain a great night’s sleep.
- [gold:relic]Joss Paper[/gold]: Mysterious sheets of incense paper… what could it be for?
- [gold:potion]Heart of Iron[/gold]: This is also in the first game but I wanted to show off how a classic potion got an art do-over :D
- [gold:potion]Ship in a Bottle[/gold]: We’re not quite sure if you’re drinking this one, but there’s a feeling that it can get you to safety.
- [gold:potion]Foul Potion[/gold]: We’re not sure who or what is making a potion called Foul Potion. What is it for? How foul is it!?

Okay, we hope you enjoyed this sneak peek.
