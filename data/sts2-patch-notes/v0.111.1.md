# Major Update #3 - v0.111.1

The third major main branch update is here, bringing all of the content, art, balance changes, and bug fixes from v0.108.0-v0.111.0. This update is an extra pretty one: unique VFX for characters' forms, special low-health idle animations, passive [gold]Orb[/gold] VFX, lots of epoch art, and more. [gold:monster]Aeonglass[/gold] is alive, with the animations shown in the July Neowsletter now available to all players. Other major additions are Traditional Chinese and Indonesian, a Keyboard-Only mode, new Neow relics, a big batch of character-specific multiplayer cards, and tons of balance changes.

## Content & Balance

### General

- End-of-turn [gold]Orb[/gold] passives now trigger before [gold:power]Doom[/gold]. If a player would die to self-[gold:power]Doom[/gold] (ex. from the [gold:card]Neurosurge[/gold] card) but also has an [gold]Orb[/gold] with an end-of-turn passive that would kill the last remaining enemy (ex. [gold]Lightning[/gold] [gold]Orb[/gold] while the enemy is at 3 HP), they now win the combat instead of losing it after ending their turn.
- Added a Randomize button to the Custom Run screen. It randomly selects 2 good modifiers and 1 bad one, and randomizes the selected character.
