# 베타 패치 노트 - v0.114.0

[gold:character]풀[/gold] 중심의 베타 패치입니다. 많은 리워크와 버프·너프, 완전히 새로운 카드 한 장, 몇 가지 아트 추가, 그리고 수많은 버그 수정이 포함됩니다.

[devnote:en]Thank you for all of your feedback over the past couple of weeks on our BIG beta patch! This beta patch is a mostly Fool-centric one, with lots of reworks, changes, nerfs, buffs, and even a brand new card! There are also a few art additions and tons of bug fixes. We'll be focusing more on polishing up Dread in the future, so keep the feedback coming![/devnote]
