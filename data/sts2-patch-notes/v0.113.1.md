# Beta Hotfix Patch Notes - v0.113.1

A small update for some quick fixes, some icons for Dread, and a translation update.

## Art

- Added icons for the following [gold]Dread[/gold] powers:
  - [gold:power]Aeonglass Dominion[/gold]
  - [gold:power]Explosive[/gold]
  - [gold:power]Insatiable Dominion[/gold]
  - [gold:power]Lagavulin Matriarch Dominion[/gold]
  - [gold:power]You Are Mine[/gold]
- [gold]Arrows[/gold] now stick to the body of every enemy instead of its center.

## Writing

- Polished the text of some of [gold:character]The Fool[/gold]'s epochs.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Fixed the timeline getting stuck when opening some of [gold:character]The Fool[/gold]'s epochs in Turkish.
- Viewing a damaged run in Run History no longer adds duplicate copies of it.
- Card previews no longer cover a potion's text when hovering it on an epoch's potion unlock screen.
- Removed a duplicate tooltip from the [gold:power]Insatiable Dominion[/gold].
- Fixed a typo in one of [gold:character]The Fool[/gold]'s epoch descriptions.
- Fixed the Japanese [gold]Arrow[/gold] tooltip showing broken text when you have no [gold]Arrows[/gold].
- Fixed Traditional Chinese text that always showed the plural wording.
- Updated translations and fixed broken formatting in several languages.

### [gold:character]The Fool[/gold]

- [gold:power]Stretch[/gold] now adds to the [gold]Block[/gold] from [gold:enchantment]Adroit[/gold] on cards that don't gain [gold]Block[/gold] themselves, like [gold:card]Ki Hop[/gold].
- [gold:card]Show Off+[/gold]'s upgrade preview no longer highlights its [gold:power]Aim[/gold] in green, which the upgrade does not change.
