# Beta Patch Notes - v0.111.0

This patch includes card reworks, balance changes, UX and performance improvements, official Indonesian localization, new card portraits, and low HP idle animations for all playable characters.

## Content & Balance

### Ancients

- [gold:card]Brightest Flame[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): max HP loss increased from 1 -> 2.
- [gold:relic]Beautiful Bracelet[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): now [gold]Enchants[/gold] 4 random cards instead of letting you choose the cards to [gold]Enchant[/gold].

### Enemies

- [gold:monster]Axebot[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]):
  - [gold]Hammer Uppercut[/gold] damage increased from 12(14) -> 14(18).
  - Now gains +10 Max HP each time it respawns.
  - [gold]The One-Two[/gold] damage increased from 9(10)x2 -> 10(11)x2.
- [gold:monster]Mecha Knight[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): now also deals 8(12) damage on its [gold]Flamethrower[/gold] turn.
- [gold:monster]Exoskeleton[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): HP at A8 increased from 24-28(25-29) -> 24-28(26-30).
- [gold:monster]Globe Head[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): [gold:power]Galvanic[/gold] at A9 increased from 6(6) -> 6(8).
- [gold:monster]Louse Progenitor[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): [gold:power]Strength[/gold] gain at A9 increased from 5(5) -> 5(7).
- [gold:monster]Soul Fysh[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): [gold]De-Gas[/gold] damage at A9 increased from 16(17) -> 16(18).

### Colorless Cards

- Changed [gold:card]Rend[/gold]:
  - Energy cost decreased from 2 -> 1.
  - Base damage decreased from 15(18) -> 10(12).
- Changed [gold:card]Salvo[/gold] and [gold:card]Splash[/gold]: swapped rarities. [gold:card]Splash[/gold] is now Rare and [gold:card]Salvo[/gold] is now Uncommon.

### Ironclad

- Reworked [gold:card]Expect a Fight[/gold]: Uncommon Skill, cost 2(1), "Gain 1 Energy for each Attack in your Hand. You cannot gain additional Energy this turn." -> Uncommon Skill, cost 3, "Gain 15(16) Block. Gains 5(8) additional Block for each [gold:power]Strength[/gold] you have."
- [gold:card]Forgotten Ritual[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): no longer requires a card to be [gold]Exhausted[/gold] to gain Energy.
- [gold:card]Rampage[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]):
  - Base damage increased from 9 -> 10.
  - Scaling damage increased from 5(9) -> 5(10).

### Silent

- [gold:card]Blade of Ink[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): [gold:enchantment]Inky[/gold] no longer gives additional damage (still applies [gold:power]Weak[/gold]).
- [gold:card]Mirage[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): now [gold]Exhausts[/gold] and loses [gold]Exhaust[/gold] on upgrade instead of lowering its cost from 1 -> 0.

### Regent

- [gold:card]Alignment[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): Star cost decreased from 3 -> 2.
- [gold:card]Spoils of Battle[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): [gold]Forge[/gold] increased from 5(8) -> 6(9).
- [gold:card]Refine Blade[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): [gold]Forge[/gold] decreased from 9(13) -> 8(12).
- [gold:relic]Regalite[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): [gold]Block[/gold] gain decreased from 6 -> 4.

### Necrobinder

- [gold:card]Shroud[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): [gold]Block[/gold] gain increased from 2(3) -> 3(4).
- [gold:card]Time's Up[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): no longer has [gold]Exhaust[/gold].

### Defect

- Reworked [gold:card]Hyperbeam[/gold]: "Deal 30(38) damage to ALL enemies. Lose 3 [gold:power]Focus[/gold]." -> "Deal 24(30) damage to ALL enemies. Lose 3 [gold:power]Focus[/gold] this turn."
- [gold:card]Thunder[/gold] ([green][sine]buff[/sine][/green]): damage increased from 6(8) -> 8(11).
- [gold:card]Null[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): [gold:power]Weak[/gold] application decreased from 2(3) -> 1(2).
- [gold:card]Synchronize[/gold] ([red][jitter]nerf[/jitter][/red]): [gold:power]Focus[/gold] decreased from 2(3) -> 1(2).

## Writing

- Added Indonesian localization with English fallback.

## User Experience & Interface

- [gold:ancient]Vakuu[/gold]'s [gold:relic]Jeweled Mask[/gold] now chooses a non-[gold]Innate[/gold] Power unless all Powers in the player's deck are [gold]Innate[/gold].
- Cards with on-end-turn effects are now played faster when there are many of them.
- The Invite button is shown when the overlay is disabled, and displays an error.
- Players can confirm card selection via E again.
- Sped up Epoch unlock VFX when playing in fast mode.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Saves are no longer copied to the modded save directory if a player declined mod loading, but left their mods installed.
- Fixed an issue where unmodded saves were not copied for players who had previously declined mod loading.
- Fixed input being blocked after clearing map drawings while in map drawing mode.
- Fixed map drawings breaking when lines are cleared while a stroke is in progress.
- Fixed a crash that could happen in multiplayer when selecting a relic at a treasure room.
- Fixed a crash that could occasionally happen after closing and then re-opening the single-card inspection screen.
- Fixed an error when a card finished moving to a pile after combat ended.
- Fixed an error when a combat would end while cards were still shuffling.
- Fixed an issue where badges earned at the end of a run could be lost if you left the game over screen early or had already earned every unlock.
- Fixed focus not returning to cards after rerolling rewards on controller.

### Enemies

- Fixed Form VFX not flipping when the player flips direction during the [gold:encounter]Kaiser Crab[/gold] combat.
- Fixed [gold:encounter]Lost and Forgotten[/gold] death particles continuing to play when the animation is interrupted in the Bestiary.

### Defect

- Fixed [aqua]Defect[/aqua]'s power-up animation freezing when interrupted while playing [gold:card]Echo Form[/gold].

### Multiplayer

- [gold:card]Cacophony[/gold] no longer triggers a second time if its first trigger causes another card to be drawn (for example via [gold:relic]Gremlin Horn[/gold]).
- Unreadying in a loaded Daily Run lobby no longer leaves you readied up.
- Fixed a multiplayer crash that could happen when killing the last enemy in an encounter with [gold:card]The Ball[/gold].

## Performance

- Reduced hitch on [gold:event]Punch Off[/gold] and [gold:event]The Lantern Key[/gold].
- Reduced stuttering when text appears for the first time in Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Thai, and Russian.
- Preloaded only the glyphs the bold and italic faces can render.
