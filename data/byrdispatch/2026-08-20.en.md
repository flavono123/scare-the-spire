# 2026-08-20

## Toy Box (General)

- Comments can now be 200 characters, not 30.
- Every service feed, including Stories, can be sorted by Latest, Recommended, or Comments.

### Defragment (new)

![Defragment one-line board](/images/byrdispatch/2026-08-20/defragment-index.png)

- Each Toy Box service is a post type, and every post gathers on a one-line board.
- When writing, pick a type and add a body the original services did not have. Comments and likes stay linked to the original post.

### Transfigure

![Cold Mittens](/images/byrdispatch/2026-08-20/transfigure-cold-mittens.png)

- Relics, potions, powers, and more can be transfigured with Slay the Spire 2 theme-color filters. (new)
- Relics now use the same stone-slab asset as the game.

### History Course (Beta)

- The current-deck view in the top bar now sorts like the game, and shows card upgrades and enchantments.
- Fixed some cards not appearing in the deck, including [gold:card]Hellraiser[/gold]. (bug) (thanks for the report)
